# Live-mode Citations cite operative provisions only, and the eval drops strength agreement

The 2026-09-03 Live run (`glm53-v5-2026-09-03`) showed both halves of the eval contract measuring the wrong thing at once: precision fell to 0.26 on bank-cloud-outage and telecom-chatbot, with DORA Recitals 13 of 27 produced targets on the bank scenario (the ground truth never cites Recitals, so every one is an automatic miss) and Answers burying definite obligations under conditionals the Scenario text already settles — "if the company were a financial entity…" for a stated financial entity — or emitting whole regimes the Scenario's facts exclude, like the full DORA stack for an online retailer — while the strength-agreement component, mean 0.42 on the 2026-08-31 run, said nothing about whether the system works: it compared the operator's per-provision ratings against the Summarizer's rated Citation strength (ADR-0011), a category error between two different axes — how load-bearing a provision is for the Scenario versus how central it is to the Answer — so it mostly measured the Summarizer's scale-usage habits (~90% of produced ratings strong). Strength agreement therefore leaves the eval as deletion — the comparison function, the per-case field, the mean aggregate, the artifact keys, the checkpoint case-record shape, and the CLI's per-case and aggregate lines — and ADR-0010's contract shrinks to its two never-blended components: provision coverage, still strength-weighted on the expected side over the operator's ratings (strong = 50, moderate = 5, weak = 1, so a missed strong provision still costs fifty times a missed framing one), and summary fidelity. The rating feature survives: ADR-0011's Summarizer-rated Citation strength stays on the Citations and in the audit dump for the human review, its calibration owned by #67's rubric change — the metric goes, not the rating. Parent spec: #68.

Live-mode Citations cite operative provisions only: both store read legs (ADR-0012's vector and lexical paths) push down a provision-kind exclusion into the SQL WHERE clause, so a Recital Chunk never consumes vector leg depth, lexical leg depth, or Evidence pool seats, and operative provisions take those seats instead. The retriever seam re-checks the exclusion on both legs — the same pushed-down-plus-re-check pattern as the relevance floor, which only the vector leg can carry because lexical matches bear no similarity score — so a store that ignores the pushed-down kinds still cannot seat a Recital; that re-check is what makes the guarantee provable offline against an adversarial store. Recitals stay ingested, and the Corpus inventory already hides them from the Planner (recitals carry no titles), so the read-path filter closes the only remaining entry path; Demo mode serves fixed content and retrieves nothing, so the canonical ground truth that cites a GDPR Recital is grandfathered untouched. The glossary's Citation definition — a reference to one provision, be it Article, Recital, or Annex — names the object, not the Live-mode contract: Recitals stay valid Citation targets in the data shape and in that grandfathered Demo ground truth, while Live mode cites operative provisions only. The workflow rubrics carry the third half, resolving Scenario facts: the Planner plans Research targets only for Regulations the Scenario's facts do not exclude and adds one Applicability target per plausibly-implicated but unconfirmed Regulation, so the Perimeter provision becomes citable for an Exclusion Finding; the Researcher drafts definite claims, resolving what the Scenario text states about the company's nature, roles, and jurisdiction into the claim itself; the Verifier judges a Scenario-settled contingency on the stated facts alone — supported only as stated, or unsupported, out of scope. The glossary's moderate-Strength and Action definitions already turned on facts the Scenario text leaves open; the code now follows them, so glossary, rubrics, and this ADR tell one story.

## Considered options

- **Keep the metric and recalibrate the produced side** — rejected: #67's calibration fixes the all-strong bias within the Summarizer's own axis but cannot make the two ratings comparable; a scored agreement between different axes is noise with authority.
- **Re-author the ground truth to expect Recitals** — rejected: the eval's authoring convention is operative provisions, and teaching ground truth to match production's recital padding would measure the padding as a feature; the Demo canonical case citing a GDPR Recital is grandfathered, not touched.
- **Filter Recitals in the Planner or the Corpus inventory only** — rejected: both already hid them, yet retrieval still seated them in the Evidence pool; the exclusion has to hold at the store read path to free the seats, and the seam re-check is what lets an offline test prove it.
- **A dedicated structural scope-resolution pipeline stage** — rejected in favour of the rubric-level gate: the Verifier already judges every claim against the Scenario, so a new stage is machinery to revisit only if the re-run still shows conditional drift.

## Consequences

- Checkpoint files written by the previous case-record shape refuse as unreadable with the standard ADR-0013 guidance (delete or resume under a different run id): the shape lost a field, and a quietly mixed measurement is worse than no measurement.
- Per-scenario precision/recall/F1 keep their shapes apart from the removed field, so the operator's comparison scripts survive; the produced Citation-strength ratings remain in the audit dump for eyeballing the (now-calibrated) all-strong bias manually.
- The Live eval is re-run manually by the operator, with directional acceptance: zero Recital targets in the produced set, mean precision clearly up, and no scenario's F1 materially regressed.
- Accepted residual risk: GPAI branch selection (systemic-risk versus base model-provider duties — the telecom run analysed Article 55/Annex 13 where the ground truth expects Article 53/Annex 11/12) is not directly addressed; duty-area enumeration may move it. Re-check on the re-run before considering a targeted heuristic.
- Neither decision is to be quietly reversed: reinstating a scored agreement metric or letting a Recital back into the Live Evidence pool takes a new ADR, not a code change.
