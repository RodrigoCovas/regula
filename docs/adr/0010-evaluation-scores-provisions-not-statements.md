# Evaluation scores provisions, not statements

The 2026-08-28 Live eval returned precision 0.000 on all 11 cases while every run succeeded and produced Findings: the semantic statement matcher (token-set cosine, threshold 0.43) paired almost nothing — it was calibrated on a single case with a 0.01 margin below the measured paraphrase floor, and it cannot bridge hand-authored, provision-dense ground truth and the Live LLM's verbose findings. Statement similarity therefore leaves the scoring path entirely. The eval reports three separate components, never blended: (1) provision coverage — deterministic set F1 over citation targets, strength-weighted on the expected side; (2) summary fidelity — a strict rubric judge comparing provision-aligned Provision relevance (same role, same obligation direction; contradiction forces the floor score); (3) strength agreement under the max-rule Citation strength, at small weight. Produced-vs-expected statements survive only as diagnostic material for the audit. This retires the semantic-matcher scoring path recorded in ADR-0001's updates.

## Consequences

- Ground truth grows: each case's cited provisions need a hand-authored relevance summary (operator work, #7 precedent — the coding agent never writes them).
- Precision over provisions is pessimistic by construction: a produced citation outside the hand-authored expected set is not necessarily wrong; the audit reviews the spurious list before numbers are quoted.
- The eval gains a second LLM (the judge, same configured provider) and with it run-to-run variance; the strict rubric and schema-validated verdicts bound it.

Update 2026-08-30 (#51): the ground-truth growth landed, and the case suite changed with it. The maintainer hand-authored every case's relevance summaries into `data/regulations/citations.json` (244 provisions across 10 cases; the coding agent never writes them), and the harness transcribes them verbatim under a pinning test against the operator's file — the operator's record stays the source, so a transcription drift fails loudly. At the maintainer's instruction the previous evaluation scenarios were discarded wholesale: the Live eval (`backend/src/live_eval.py`) now runs exactly the 10 operator-authored cases, each describing its own Scenario, with citations grouped into hand-authored Findings following the authoring conventions recorded in `data/PROVENANCE.md`. Summary fidelity therefore measures on every case, with the deterministic floor where the Summarizer ships nothing. The three canonical Demo cases leave the Live run and stay what ADR-0001 makes them: Demo-mode functional tripwires, unchanged.

Update 2026-08-31 (#55–#58): the expected side's Strength source moved to the operator's per-provision ratings, and the hand-authored Finding-level Strengths are deleted. The Finding-level Strengths the harness carried disagreed with the operator's ratings on most provisions (9 of 11 in the first Scenario); keeping two disagreeing records invited silent drift, so — the Citations having carried the transcribed ratings since #56 and fed every score since #57 — the Finding-level field is removed from the data shape, the ten Live Scenario blocks, the Demo canonical copy, and every test helper. The operator's citations record is the single source of expected Citation strength. Coverage recall weights each expected provision strong = 50, moderate = 5, weak = 1, replacing the old 3/2/1: under the operator's distribution (42 strong / 44 moderate / 158 weak across the 244 provisions), 3/2/1 let the 158 weak, framing-only provisions nearly cancel the strong ones in the recall denominator, while 50/5/1 makes a missed provision that names the situation outright cost fifty times a missed framing provision — recall becomes effectively a strong-provision measure, matching the operator's judgment of each provision's directness. Strength agreement's expected half reads the same ratings through the one shared max-rule implementation, so product and eval aggregation cannot diverge; precision stays unweighted, and the three components stay separate.

Update 2026-09-03 (#68, ADR-0014): the third component above, strength agreement, leaves the scoring path as deletion — the comparison function, the per-case field, the mean aggregate, the artifact keys, and the CLI's per-case and aggregate lines. Once ADR-0011 made the produced side rated rather than derived, the component became a category error — the operator's ratings measure a provision's centrality to the Scenario while the Summarizer's measure its centrality to the Answer, different axes whose agreement measured the Summarizer's scale-usage habits, not substance. The eval reports the two remaining components, never blended: the operator's ratings still weight coverage's recall through the expected-side max-rule derivation above, and the Summarizer's rated Citation strength stays on the Citations and in the audit dump, unscored (its calibration lives in #67).
