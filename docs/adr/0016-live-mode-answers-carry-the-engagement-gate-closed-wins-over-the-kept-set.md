# Live-mode answers carry the engagement gate: closed-wins over the kept set, duty-area Findings dropped with recorded reasons

The glm53-v7 run (2026-09-04, regenerated 2026-09-06) showed the pipeline's engagement policy inverted in both directions at once. Duty limbs were produced whose conditions the Scenario's facts settle the other way — DORA incident-regime limbs for an online retailer, high-risk limbs for a transparency-tier chatbot, a provider serious-incident regime for a firm that is not a provider — which is why the three worst cases score telecom P 0.361, retailer P 0.538, ransomware P 0.737; while the genuinely open conditions got dropped, the fintech case's whole conditional DORA block vanishing even though its entity-status question is the one thing the scenario leaves undecided. The prompt-side rules (the Verifier's settled-contingency case, ADR-0015) cannot close this deterministically: the run shows such limbs surviving verification, and where the kept set holds both an exclusion claim and an open engagement claim over the same provision, something must resolve the conflict the same way every time.

So the claim-decision step gains the engagement gate — deterministic application code, after verification and after the reserved-anchor merge (issue #84: the gate over-deletes without anchors), never trusted to the LLM. Per Regulation the kept Findings cite, the Engagement state computes from the kept set alone: *open* when some kept Finding cites one of the Regulation's Perimeter provisions as applying or as an open question, *closed* when a kept Finding cites such a provision as not reaching the Scenario; both present resolves **closed-wins**, the conflict recorded in the detailed trace. A kept Finding whose scope is only closed Regulations — and which itself cites no Perimeter provision — is rejected with a recorded reason, mirroring every other recorded rejection; the Perimeter Citation surfaces as the Exclusion Finding, the expected form. The perimeter provisions come from each corpus document's own metadata declaration — DORA Article 2, GDPR Articles 2, 3, and 4, AI Act Articles 2 and 6 — corpus-level knowledge, not case knowledge, so no Scenario is hard-coded anywhere: the gate reads Citations, corpus metadata, and the statements' deterministic exclusion forms, nothing else. The Verifier's instructions gain the worked within-regime Annex III example alongside this ADR — a qualifying use the Scenario never describes is unsupported, a contingency the text genuinely leaves open stays moderate — the prompt side of the same discipline. The glossary gains Engagement state and Duty-area finding.

## Considered options

- **Trusting the Verifier's verdicts to settle engagement** — rejected: the run shows excluded-branch duty limbs surviving verification, and the repo's standing rule is that enforcement lives in application code; the same kept set must resolve conflicting evidence deterministically.
- **Case hard-coding** (per-Scenario tables, per-case overrides) — rejected: ten curated eval keys is exactly where hard-coding rots; the gate's inputs are Citations, the corpus metadata's perimeter declarations, and statement text.
- **Open-wins on conflicting evidence** — rejected: an Answer carrying both directions is the pathology; where the facts genuinely leave the question open the conditional block survives (the fintech tripwire pins this), and where the kept set holds both, closed-wins with the conflict recorded is the reading a professional gives settled-away reach.
- **A dedicated structural engagement stage** — rejected in favour of the claim-decision gate, as in ADR-0014 and ADR-0015: the decision already has one application-code derivation point (the Proposer's, #47's design); a new stage is machinery to revisit only if the post-run analysis shows the seam wrong.

## Consequences

- The Answer's shape changes user-visibly: a closed regime contributes its Exclusion Finding — carrying the Perimeter Citation — and no duty limbs; a genuinely open regime keeps its conditional block; an undecided regime (no kept perimeter citation) is untouched.
- The corpus documents declare their own perimeter provisions; a document without a declaration, or with a malformed one, degrades the gate to undecided — fewer drops, never a request-path error. The curated table is pinned by test against the shipped metadata.
- The direction read keys on the taught exclusion forms (the Verifier rubric's "does not reach" family, the scope-exit phrases, the negated financial-entity term, the allocation form); an exclusion phrased some other way slips past the gate and shows up as a missed firing in the rejection trace and the ledger, not as a wrong answer.
- The gate runs after the reserved-anchor merge and before the Proposer's labelling, so a dropped Finding never anchors an Action nor feeds the Summarizer, and its rejection rides the same `claim_decisions` record every other rejection does.
- The Live eval is re-run manually by the operator under a fresh run id; directional acceptance: the three worst cases recover precision (telecom, retailer, ransomware) and the fintech conditional DORA block survives.
- None of this is to be quietly reversed: changing closed-wins to open-wins, dropping the recorded-reason requirement, moving the gate into a prompt-side rule, or editing the corpus perimeter declarations without recording it takes a new ADR, not a code change.
