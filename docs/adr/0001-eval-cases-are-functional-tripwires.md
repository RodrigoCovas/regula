# Evaluation cases are functional tripwires until the LLM/RAG pipeline

The strength-weighted scorer (`backend/src/eval_harness.py`) is real, but while the demo is deterministic and ground truth is transcribed from the same locked content, mean F1 is 1.0 by construction: the curated cases pin contracts (routing leakage, strength tagging, demo-content drift) rather than measure answer quality. We decided to leave these simple cases as-is for the MVP, and to introduce quality evaluation only with the LLM/RAG pipeline, in a fixed order: author curated ground truth (the manual work on #7) → replace exact statement-string matching with semantic matching → add citation-fidelity scoring → only then grow the case suite into sophisticated quality cases.

## Consequences

- Exact statement-string matching and unscored citations began as deliberate MVP simplifications, not oversights. Steps 2 and 3 of the fixed sequence have since landed as scoring machinery (see the dated updates below) — but nothing resembling quality evaluation happens before #7: a paraphrase-tolerant matcher and a fidelity scorer still have nothing real to judge while ground truth is transcribed from verbatim-by-construction output.
- Any drop below F1 = 1.0 today is the intended signal: it means a regression (reworded Finding, retagged Strength, or routing leak onto non-canonical ids), not poor quality.

Update 2026-08-24 (#12): the semantic-matching scorer landed early as **machinery only** — step 2 of this sequence was built and proven on synthetic cases while #7's ground truths are still outstanding. The curated suite is unaffected: every case keeps verbatim matching (the default), so F1 = 1.0 stays the regression signal until a hand-authored ground-truth case explicitly opts into `semantic_matcher`. Threshold calibration and matcher validation against real expectations remain gated on #7.

Update 2026-08-24 (#13): citation-fidelity scoring landed as **machinery only** — step 3 of the sequence, proven on synthetic cases like step 2 before it. Matched-Finding credit now scales by structural agreement between expected and produced Citation targets (same source_id and provision kind + number, parsed from ground-truth labels; never compared by label string) — missed, mistargeted, and extra produced Citations all lower it. The curated suite needs no opt-out flag: Demo production derives its Citations from the same locked targets its ground truth transcribes, so demo fidelity is 1.0 while every expected target still resolves in the Corpus — a target the Corpus stops supplying lowers F1 loudly, which is exactly this tripwire's intended signal. Calibration against real expectations remains gated on #7's hand-authored ground truths.

Update 2026-08-28: the first Live eval through the semantic matcher returned precision 0.000 on all 11 cases — statement similarity could not bridge the hand-authored, provision-dense ground truth and the Live LLM's verbose output. The matcher's scoring role is retired in favour of provision-level scoring (see ADR-0010); the machinery remains only as diagnostic material.

Update 2026-08-29 (#46): the retirement landed as deletion. The scoring machinery from the updates above — semantic pairing (#12), matched-Finding credit with citation fidelity (#13), and verbatim pairing before them — is removed from `eval_harness.py`; scoring is provision coverage over Citation targets (the first of ADR-0010's three never-blended components; summary fidelity and strength agreement follow in #50). Statements survive only as diagnostic material: the produced-versus-expected dump the Live eval CLI writes for the audit. The Demo tripwire is unchanged in kind: Demo production derives its Citations from the same locked targets its ground truth transcribes, so coverage F1 is 1.0 by construction, and a target the Corpus stops supplying still lowers the score loudly.

Update 2026-09-11 (#54, #105): the demo content swapped to the bank cloud outage case, the best-performing Live eval Scenario. The exact-match demo trigger id is now `bank-cloud-outage`; the demo Finding defs follow the authored bank-case gold (13 statements), with every Citation target's relevance summary and per-provision strength rating transcribed verbatim from the operator's citations record — the strengths ride the Citations alone (ADR-0011), and a pinning test now extends to the demo defs so drift from the record fails CI. The tripwire property is unchanged in kind: the canonical cases re-point at the new id with gold transcribed from the citations record as an independent source, deliberately not derived from the demo defs (#7 precedent — the eval can still disagree with the code), Demo production still derives its Citations from the same locked targets, and coverage F1 stays 1.0 by construction; a drop in CI after the swap is a regression signal, never quality. The near-miss probe becomes a look-alike of the new id; the unrelated-id and empty-id probes keep their contracts (ADR-0005). Definition hashes of the edited eval cases change, so existing Live-eval checkpoints refuse and the next operator run starts fresh under a new run id (ADR-0013) — expected.
